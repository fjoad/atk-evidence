Study 2 — Graph transfer learning for water-network attack detection

Ahasan, Joad, Atat, Thompson, Serpedin, Takiddin — EUSIPCO 2025.

Verdict: no consistent protocol. An exhaustive pre-registered search over 67,326 measurement protocols per cell — every test-set size from 20 to 1000, every class prevalence from 2% to 98%, and six metric-definition variants — finds no single protocol that reproduces the reported table. 20 of 27 cells are unreachable under every protocol searched, and the best single protocol explains 1 cell out of 27. Independently of the numbers, the stated mechanism does not hold: the task decomposes into one class needing no model, one class the paper’s own detector cannot express, and one class undetectable in principle.
Disclosure. One of this project’s maintainers is a co-author of this paper. That is why the audit is held to pre-registered ambiguity branches, published corrections of its own errors, and verdicts bounded strictly to what the artefact supports. This page does not assert how the reported numbers arose.

1. The reported numbers are not realisable

On the paper’s own balanced 50/50 split, ACC = (DR + SP)/2 with SP ≤ 100%, hence ACC ≤ (DR+100)/2.

One-directional error excludes rounding and isolated transcription error, which would scatter both ways. The attainability checker was validated against 15 constructed confusion matrices and accepted all 15. Worst case: LSTM at 31 nodes reports accuracy 64.7% where its own DR and F1 imply 10.0%.

A forecaster supplied with frozen history predicts the frozen value, so the residual vanishes — the paper’s detector cannot express the very test that catches denial of service almost perfectly.

3. Two trivial rules match the whole architecture

A composite of the two zero-parameter rules reaches F1 71.5 / ACC 76.9 / DR 60.5 at FA 7.9%, with no parameters and no seed variance. Trained models reach F1 ≈ 70 at a higher false-alarm rate when trained on benign windows only — and only 44–46 under the paper’s own literal prescription that every split carry equal samples per class. Under the paper’s stated training reading, every trained model scores below the zero-parameter composite. Every baseline the paper reports — SVM 6.1, RF 17.5, LGBM 19.8, FFNN 20.9, AE 21.5, LSTM 26.0 — falls below a detector with no parameters.

4. The stated mechanism fails on its own terms

The paper attributes its architecture to spatial and temporal structure being too difficult for a feed-forward network. Varying capacity across a 272× parameter range leaves performance flat:

The network is not capacity-limited, so capacity was never the missing ingredient. Structure does exist (cross-sensor correlation 0.18–0.24, lag-one temporal correlation 0.65–0.74 on attack-free windows) — the premise is not empty. What fails is the inference that exploiting it requires this architecture.

5. Replay is undetectable in principle

Replay reproduces genuine past readings, so a replayed window is a real benign window. Nearest-neighbour distance from held-out test windows to a bank of attack-free training windows:

With replay undetectable, detection rate is bounded near 1 − replay share — 57.1% under the attack mix used here, against a reported 74.7%. The paper does not state its attack mix, so this bound is conditional on the mix.

6. Stability

Across five seeds on identical hardware, software and data, TGCN spans 23.7 F1 points and STGT 11.8; feed-forward and recurrent baselines stay within 2–8. STGT’s denial-of-service detection alone swings from 5.8% to 95.8%. The paper reports single values to one decimal with no dispersion.

7. Where the paper's premise holds

Reported because it disconfirms this audit’s own expectation. The graph topology carries real signal. Shuffling the C-Town adjacency (same edges, permuted labels) or removing edges costs up to 18 F1: STGT at 10 nodes scores 57.9 real / 51.9 shuffled / 44.9 identity; TL-STGT at 31 nodes 63.6 / 45.2 / 44.7. The graph is not decorative.

Transfer learning shows a large gain at 31 nodes, +13.3 to +21.9 F1 over scratch STGT across seeds. Two caveats: the comparison is budget-confounded (TL-STGT is pretrained on the smaller graphs, so it sees roughly 3× the training), and under benign-only training the gain collapses from +21.9 to +3.1 — so what transfer buys is mostly robustness to the paper’s attack-corrupted training prescription, not better detection.

8. Configuration matters more than any model choice

The paper states every split carries equal samples per class, so half the training data is attack-corrupted. Training on benign windows instead moves F1 at 31 nodes by roughly 25 points:

Corrections to this audit

Published because a reviewer finding them independently would be fatal, and because a project that hides its own errors cannot credibly report anyone else’s.

  1. Retracted — a reported 46-point shortfall against the paper. Substantially caused by a defect in our threshold selection, which drew reference samples from validation windows contaminated by adjacent attacks and inflated the threshold roughly 8,000×.
  2. Retracted — attributing an identical-metrics collapse to the scale incoherence of δ. Corrected runs show the threshold defect alone produced it.
  3. Corrected — a structure measurement reading ≈0 temporal correlation, which would have supported “there is no temporal structure.” It used attack-contaminated history; on attack-free windows the correlation is 0.67.
  4. Corrected — a nearest-neighbour test whose reference bank included the samples being evaluated, which matched themselves at distance zero and inverted the comparison.
  5. Corrected — an earlier draft of this page quoted benign-training results without saying so, while the paper’s literal reading trains on the balanced split. The two differ by ~25 F1. Every figure now carries its configuration.
  6. Corrected — this audit expected the graph topology to carry no signal. It does (§7). Reported against expectation.

Limitations

The benign series is the canonical DeepH2O C-Town series, not the paper’s own unpublished 1,400-hour run. Attack duration, replay offset, window length, and the shallow classifiers’ detection rule are not specified by the paper and are documented assumptions. The attack mix is ours, which conditions the ceiling in §5. The pre-registered interpretation sweep is incomplete.
