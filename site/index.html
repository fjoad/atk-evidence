Independent reproducibility audit

Published numbers should follow from published methods.

ATK Evidence is an audit pipeline for published results that do not look right. Papers enter, get rebuilt from scratch against the printed method, and leave with a verdict — including harsh ones, where the evidence supports them.

Most reproduction work asks “do the numbers come out?” That question is too weak: it invites the reply “you implemented it wrong” — and sometimes that reply is correct. We audit a paper the way a reviewer would who doubts every item in it and checks each one personally.
Conflict of interest. A maintainer of this project is the second author of the 2025 water paper audited as study 2. That verdict is held to pre-registered ambiguity branches, published corrections of the audit’s own errors, and conclusions bounded strictly to what the artefact supports.

The pipeline

Tiers are ordered so a failure low down makes everything above it moot. A project that only runs the last tier can spend months reproducing a result that never needed a model.

  1. Does the paper cohere with itself? No computation. Do the reported numbers satisfy the metric definitions they claim?
  2. Is the problem non-trivial? Measure a zero-parameter rule through the identical evaluation path first. If it performs comparably, every downstream comparison is moot — including ours.
  3. Is each claimed component doing work? Destroy one claimed structure at a time and see whether anything notices.
  4. Are the comparisons fair? If our build of the paper’s own baseline beats the paper’s reported baseline, the margin was measured against something undertrained.
  5. Is the evaluation sound? Per-class detectability, seed variance, threshold sensitivity.
  6. Only now, the headline claims — with intervals, not point estimates.

The verdict ladder

VerdictMeaning
reproducedThe complete reported result pattern was recovered.
partially reproducedSome reported results recovered, others not.
not reproducedNot recovered within the finite tested space. Bounded by that space — not a claim that reproduction is impossible.
internally inconsistentThe reported numbers contradict each other or the paper’s own stated protocol, independent of any implementation.
unattainable as printedNo valid confusion matrix on the described protocol yields the printed numbers, and the deviation is systematic rather than consistent with rounding or isolated transcription error.
no consistent protocolStrongest rung. An exhaustive pre-registered search over measurement protocols — any test-set size, any class prevalence, and the common metric-definition variants — finds no single protocol reproducing the reported table, and most cells are unreachable under every protocol searched. The likelihood of the table under “these were measured” is then zero rather than small.

Every rung is a statement about the artefact. Even the strongest does not distinguish deliberate fabrication from catastrophic process failure — placeholder values never replaced, a table carried from a draft — because both produce numbers that were never measured. That distinction requires evidence outside the paper, and this project does not assert it.

Rules we hold ourselves to

  1. Reconstruct the method from the paper before treating any prior implementation as authority.
  2. Execute questionable printed steps rather than silently correcting them.
  3. Enumerate every material ambiguity before results are seen.
  4. Design experiments whose outcome we do not control. Results confirming a paper’s premise are reported as plainly as results that do not.
  5. No cherry-picking: distributions and failures, never the best seed.
  6. Publish our own errors. Each study page carries a corrections section. Two findings in study 2 were retracted as our own bugs.
This project assesses reproducibility within documented finite experiment spaces. It does not infer author intent. Source code and evidence are at github.com/fjoad/atk-evidence.
