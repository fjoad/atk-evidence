Independent paper-by-paper evidence audit

Published numbers and explanations should follow from published methods.

ATK Evidence rebuilds papers whose results do not look right and asks three separate questions: did the number reproduce, did the claimed mechanism explain the advantage, and does the target lie inside a credible empirical performance envelope?

“Do the numbers come out?” is necessary but not sufficient. A correct number can come from the wrong mechanism, and a failed reproduction does not prove that every possible implementation must fail. Numerical, mechanism, and attainability findings therefore have separate evidence bars.
Conflict of interest. A maintainer of this project is the second author of the 2025 water paper audited as study 2. Those findings are held to pre-registered ambiguity branches, published corrections of the audit’s own errors, and conclusions bounded strictly to what the artefact supports.

The pipeline

After one complete orientation read, we explore cheaply in a disposable sandbox, return to the paper to freeze the formal reading, and promote only questions that survive diagnostic breadth to expensive execution.

  1. Read and map the claims. Record the numerical targets and causal story, such as “B beats A because component Z exploits structure S.”
  2. Discover in a sandbox. Use toy witnesses, trivial rules, minimal models, static checks, and output inspection to find tests that distinguish competing explanations.
  3. Freeze the source reading. Return to the complete paper and predeclare the executable method, reasonable interpretations, predictions, budget, and stopping rules.
  4. Build the minimal instrument. Acquire the exact data, implement the paper-literal path, and prove the data, model, and metric routes on tiny cases.
  5. Run diagnostic breadth. Check coherence, triviality, claimed structure, component necessity, fairness, evaluation soundness, and possible ceilings with the cheapest informative probe first.
  6. Promote surviving questions. Only then spend deeply on numerical branches, capability-sensitive mechanism tests, or an empirical performance envelope.
  7. Confirm and report. Preserve all attempts and issue three bounded findings rather than one overloaded verdict.

The three findings

FindingQuestionRequired evidence
NumericalWas the reported result recovered?A source-faithful finite space, repeated runs, uncertainty, failures, and predeclared comparison criteria.
MechanismDid the claimed component or structure cause the advantage?Capability witnesses, structure controls, ablations, trivial baselines, and behavior that discriminates the claim from alternatives.
AttainabilityWhere is the target relative to the observed performance envelope?Declared search axes, distributions, response curves, plateaus, target gap, budget, and explicit untested space.

A numerical miss does not establish a mechanism failure or universal impossibility. A flat empirical curve supports bounded implausibility only inside its declared envelope. Literal impossibility is reserved for a proved constraint whose assumptions exactly match the printed protocol.

Numerical and internal-consistency vocabulary

FindingMeaning
reproducedThe complete reported result pattern was recovered.
partially reproducedSome reported results recovered, others not.
not reproducedNot recovered within the finite tested space. Bounded by that space — not a claim that reproduction is impossible.
internally inconsistentThe reported numbers contradict each other or the paper’s own stated protocol, independent of any implementation.
unattainable as printedNo valid confusion matrix on the described protocol yields the printed numbers, and the deviation is systematic rather than consistent with rounding or isolated transcription error.
no consistent protocolStrongest rung. An exhaustive pre-registered search over measurement protocols — any test-set size, any class prevalence, and the common metric-definition variants — finds no single protocol reproducing the reported table, and most cells are unreachable under every protocol searched. The likelihood of the table under “these were measured” is then zero rather than small.

Every label is a statement about the artefact and the declared evidence space. Even the strongest does not distinguish deliberate fabrication from catastrophic process failure. That distinction requires evidence outside the paper, and this project does not assert it.

Rules we hold ourselves to

  1. Read the complete paper once, explore adaptively in a visibly disposable sandbox, then return to the paper and freeze the formal method.
  2. Treat author code as an artefact, not authority over unclear or contradictory printed wording.
  3. Execute questionable printed steps rather than silently correcting them.
  4. Enumerate every material formal interpretation before its eligible results are seen.
  5. Test competing explanations cheaply before expanding model families or search spaces.
  6. Design experiments whose outcome we do not control. Results confirming a paper’s premise are reported as plainly as results that do not.
  7. No cherry-picking: distributions and failures, never the best seed.
  8. Keep numerical, mechanism, and attainability conclusions separate and bounded to the evidence that earned them.
  9. Publish our own errors. Each study page carries a corrections section. Two findings in study 2 were retracted as our own bugs.
This project assesses numerical reproduction, claimed mechanisms, and empirical attainability within documented finite evidence spaces. It does not infer author intent or claim to exhaust undocumented procedures. Source code and evidence are at github.com/fjoad/atk-evidence.
